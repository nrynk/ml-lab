================================================================================
Experiment 10: Implement and Compare Logistic Regression and Decision Trees on Adult Income Dataset
================================================================================

AIM:
Implement and compare Logistic Regression and Decision Trees on the Adult Income dataset for predicting income levels (>50K vs <=50K). Evaluate both models based on performance metrics and interpretability.

ALGORITHM / PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Load Adult Income dataset ('adult.csv').
Step 2: Handle missing values ('?' -> NaN -> dropna).
Step 3: Encode categorical variables using LabelEncoder.
Step 4: Split into 80% training and 20% testing sets.
Step 5: Train Logistic Regression model (max_iter=1000).
Step 6: Train Decision Tree model (criterion='entropy', max_depth=5).
Step 7: Evaluate both models using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-score.
Step 8: Compare models and analyze linear vs hierarchical decision-making interpretability.
