================================================================================
Experiment 20: Bias-Variance Tradeoff using Polynomial Regression
================================================================================

AIM:
Investigate the bias-variance tradeoff using polynomial regression on housing price data. Plot training and validation errors for various polynomial degrees (1 to 5) and discuss the tradeoff between bias and variance.

ALGORITHM / PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Load housing dataset (Boston housing or California housing) and extract single representative feature.
Step 2: Split data into 80% training and 20% validation sets.
Step 3: For polynomial degrees 1 through 5:
   - Create Pipeline with StandardScaler, PolynomialFeatures(degree), and LinearRegression.
   - Fit pipeline on training data.
   - Predict training and validation sets.
   - Compute Training MSE (Bias indicator) and Validation MSE (Variance indicator).
Step 4: Plot Training Error and Validation Error curves vs Polynomial Degree. Save to biasvariance_plot.png.
Step 5: Identify underfitting regime (high bias, low variance), optimal model complexity, and overfitting regime (low bias, high variance).
